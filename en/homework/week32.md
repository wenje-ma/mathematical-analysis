> **Basic Knowledge: Center of Mass Formula**<br>$x_0=\frac1m\iiint_\Omega x\rho\,\mathrm dV$, etc.

> **Basic Knowledge: Moment of Inertia Formula**<br>$J_z=\iiint_\Omega\left(x^2+y^2\right)\rho\,\mathrm dV$.

> **Proposition 5.1 (Surface Area Formula)**<br>$S=\iint_D\sqrt{1+f_x^2+f_y^2}\,\mathrm dx\mathrm dy$.

---

## 8.5-1

Find the area of the cone $z=\sqrt{x^2+y^2}$ cut by $z^2=2x$.

### Solution 8.5-1

The projection is a disk. Since $\sqrt{1+f_x^2+f_y^2}=\sqrt2$, by **Proposition 5.1 (Surface Area Formula)**, $S=\sqrt2\pi$. $\blacksquare$

---

## 8.5-2

Find the area of $az=xy$ inside $x^2+y^2=a^2$.

### Solution 8.5-2

$S=\frac{2\pi a^2}{3}\left(2\sqrt2-1\right)$. $\blacksquare$

---

## 8.5-3

Find the center of mass of the first-octant ellipsoid.

### Solution 8.5-3

By symmetry and **Basic Knowledge: Center of Mass Formula**, $\left(\frac{3a}{8},\frac{3b}{8},\frac{3c}{8}\right)$. $\blacksquare$

---

## 8.5-6

Find the moment of inertia of a cube about the $z$-axis.

### Solution 8.5-6

$J_z=\frac{2\rho a^5}{3}$. $\blacksquare$
