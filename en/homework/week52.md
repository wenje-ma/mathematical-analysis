> **Basic Knowledge: Inner Product and Norm**<br>$\left\langle f,g\right\rangle=\frac1\pi\int_{-\pi}^\pi f\left(x\right)g\left(x\right)\mathrm dx$, $\left|f\right|=\sqrt{\left\langle f,f\right\rangle}$.

> **Proposition (Orthogonality of Trigonometric System)**<br>$1,\cos x,\sin x,\cdots$ are pairwise orthogonal on $\left[-\pi,\pi\right]$, with $\int1=2\pi$, $\int\cos^2nx=\int\sin^2nx=\pi$.

---

## 13.1-1

Prove symmetry and linearity of the inner product.

### Solution 13.1-1

By definition and linearity of integration. $\blacksquare$

---

## 13.1-2

Prove the Cauchy-Schwarz inequality.

### Solution 13.1-2

By $\left|f-\lambda g\right|^2\ge0$ and discriminant. $\blacksquare$

---

## 13.1-3

Prove equality holds iff $f,g$ are linearly dependent.

### Solution 13.1-3

By continuity and $\left|f-\lambda_0g\right|^2=0$. $\blacksquare$

## 13.1-7

Prove linear independence of the trigonometric system.

### Solution 13.1-7

By orthogonality. $\blacksquare$
