> **Proposition (Fourier Coefficients)**<br>$f\left(x\right)\sim\frac{a_0}2+\sum\left(a_n\cos nx+b_n\sin nx\right)$, where $a_n=\frac1\pi\int_{-\pi}^\pi f\cos nx$, $b_n=\frac1\pi\int_{-\pi}^\pi f\sin nx$.

---

## 13.2-1-1

Find the Fourier series of $f\left(x\right)=x^2$.

### Solution 13.2-1-1

$x^2\sim\frac{\pi^2}3+4\sum\frac{\left(-1\right)^n}{n^2}\cos nx$. $\blacksquare$

---

## 13.2-1-3

Find the Fourier series of $\sin^4x$.

### Solution 13.2-1-3

$\sin^4x=\frac38-\frac12\cos2x+\frac18\cos4x$. $\blacksquare$
