> **Theorem 4.3 (Weierstrass M-Test for Improper Integrals)**<br>If $\left|f\left(x,y\right)\right|\le F\left(x\right)$ and $\int_a^b F$ converges, then $\int_a^b f$ converges uniformly.

> **Theorem 4.7 (Interchange of Order)**<br>Under uniform convergence and fixed sign, $\int_c^d\mathrm dy\int_a^b f=\int_a^b\mathrm dx\int_c^d f$.

---

## 12.5-1-1

Discuss uniform convergence of $\int_0^1\frac{\sin x}{x^\alpha}\mathrm dx$.

### Solution 12.5-1-1

(i) Uniformly convergent for $\alpha\le\alpha_0< 2$. (ii) Not uniformly convergent on $\alpha< 2$. $\blacksquare$

---

## 12.5-1-2

Discuss $\int_0^1 x^{p-1}\mathrm dx$.

### Solution 12.5-1-2

(i) Uniformly convergent for $p\ge p_0> 0$. (ii) Not uniformly convergent on $p> 0$. $\blacksquare$

## 12.5-2-1

Evaluate $\int_0^1\frac{x^b-x^a}{\ln x}\mathrm dx$.

### Solution 12.5-2-1

$\ln\frac{1+b}{1+a}$. $\blacksquare$
